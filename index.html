About LSM

A Professional Centre of Music Education & Performance

"Where discipline meets artistry and talent is developed into excellence."

Lilongwe School of Music (LSM) is Malawi's leading conservatory-level institution, committed to the highest standards of musical training and artistic excellence. Guided by internationally recognized frameworks, the school provides structured instruction across instrumental performance, vocal studies, music theory, and ensemble training.

LSM serves both as an academic institution and a performance hub preparing students for graded examinations while offering professional music services for a diverse range of events. Whether you are beginning your musical journey or an advanced performer seeking diploma-level qualifications, LSM offers a structured pathway to excellence.

Apply Now Contact Us

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, structured music education that develops skilled and disciplined musicians while fostering creativity and excellence. We strive to give a voice to the voiceless placing instruments in the hands of aspiring individuals and nurturing their talent through instrumental training, choir, and opera studies.

✨ Our Vision

To be the leading centre of music education and performance in Malawi and beyond recognized for producing well-rounded musicians and delivering outstanding musical experiences.

🏛️ Our Conservatory Identity

LSM is modelled on conservatory best practice rigorous curriculum, ensemble training, performance development pathways, and internationally benchmarked examinations through Trinity College London, ABRSM, and the London College of Music.

Entry Requirements
  • ✔No prior experience required for beginners all absolute beginners are welcome across all age groups.
  • ✔Previous grade certificates (if applicable) for students transferring from other institutions.
  • ✔Instrument placement assessment. A brief informal assessment to determine the appropriate starting level.

LSM Open Orchestra

An independent orchestra identity, supported by the musical foundation of Lilongwe School of Music.

About us

LSM Open Orchestra is an orchestra founded by Lilongwe School of Music. We bring musicians together to prepare and perform orchestral programmes with care, commitment and artistic purpose.

We are building the orchestra step by step: developing our musicians, establishing our sound and presenting performances for audiences in Malawi. Each project is an opportunity to make meaningful music, work well together and grow as an ensemble.

The orchestra has its own identity and artistic direction, supported by the musical foundation of Lilongwe School of Music.

Classical music is at the heart of both Lilongwe School of Music and LSM Open Orchestra. Our performances will also include contemporary music and local instruments, reflecting the musical tastes and traditions of the community.

Membership and recruitment

LSM Open Orchestra welcomes applications from musicians who are interested in orchestral performance and meet the requirements of an advertised position.

Lilongwe School of Music students will receive first consideration as the orchestra seeks to support and develop their artistry. Musicians from outside the school are also welcome to apply.

Membership applications will be invited when positions are advertised for orchestral instruments and local instruments. Interested musicians must complete the application form provided for that recruitment period and submit any requested supporting materials. Applications are reviewed for each opening, and selection depends on the instruments needed, the musical requirements and the needs of the orchestra.

Membership is renewed annually. Members who wish to continue must reapply when the annual renewal process is announced. Renewal and selection will depend on the orchestra's needs and the member's participation and readiness.

Project-based engagement: Musicians are engaged and paid on a project and performance basis. Payment relates to a specific project or performance, according to the terms communicated for that engagement. It is not a fixed hourly wage or monthly salary, and payment is not guaranteed between projects. Each project announcement or agreement will explain the payment amount, what it covers and when it will be made.

Requirements to apply

Applicants should:

  • Play an instrument named in the advertised opening.
  • Meet the musical standard stated for that position.
  • Be able to read music, where required for the repertoire.
  • Be willing to prepare parts independently and work under a conductor.
  • Attend the scheduled rehearsals and performances.
  • Demonstrate commitment, punctuality, reliability and a collaborative attitude.
  • Complete the application form and provide the requested information or recording.
  • Follow any additional requirements stated in the position advertisement.

Each recruitment notice will specify the available positions, eligibility, audition or assessment process, application deadline, rehearsal schedule, performance commitments and payment terms.

How to apply

  1. Read the current recruitment or membership renewal announcement.
  2. Complete the application form provided for that opening.
  3. Submit the form and any requested materials by the stated deadline.
  4. Attend an audition or assessment if invited or required.
  5. Wait for the orchestra to contact you about the outcome and next steps.

Our commitment

We are committed to thoughtful preparation, high standards and respect for the work of every musician. We will build the orchestra through the music we perform, the way we work together and the relationships we form with audiences and partners.

Leadership at LSM

Our leadership team supports the work of Lilongwe School of Music and LSM Open Orchestra, bringing together artistic direction, planning, operations and financial stewardship.

Artistic and Academic Lead

Brian Lia provides artistic and academic leadership across Lilongwe School of Music and LSM Open Orchestra. He guides the orchestra's musical direction and standards, supports repertoire and programme development, and contributes to the learning and artistic growth of its musicians. He is also a music teacher at Lilongwe School of Music.

Planning and Development Lead

Edgar Mwanja supports planning and development across Lilongwe School of Music and LSM Open Orchestra, including shaping projects, coordinating development priorities and building opportunities that contribute to the orchestra's growth. He is also a music teacher at Lilongwe School of Music.

Mr. Vitumbiko Chisi

Finance Lead

Vitumbiko Chisi supports financial planning and administration for Lilongwe School of Music and LSM Open Orchestra, including responsible budgeting and financial management. He also serves as a brass facilitator, contributing to the preparation and development of brass musicians.

Operations Advisor

Advises on operational planning and coordination, helping strengthen the day-to-day processes that support the school's programmes.
